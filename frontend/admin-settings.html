Deposit Address (USDT BEP20)

This is the address shown to users on their Deposit page. Update it here — no server restart or .env edit needed, and it takes effect immediately.

Principal Withdrawal Windows

Enter the month numbers (after deposit approval) at which users are allowed to withdraw their Principal. Use a single number for one window, or comma-separate multiple — e.g. 18,36 (original default), 1 (every month), 3,6,12 (quarterly, semi, annual).

Withdrawal Testing Mode

Withdrawals normally follow the real rules — Principal is only eligible at the 18th or 36th month after deposit approval, and Income only on the last day of the month. Turn this on to bypass both locks for every user so you can submit and approve test withdrawals right now. Remember to turn it back off afterwards.
